Open Source Bond Asset Pricing

Corporate bond data, factors and the code that builds them. Everything here is produced by a public pipeline from raw TRACE, Lehman and ICE data, and everything is free to download.

The paper behind this data is under Revise and Resubmit, so the data may change over the coming months. Every change is recorded in the changelog on GitHub.
Data The daily and monthly bond panels, the 108 factors, and the reports that show what the cleaning did. FactorViz Explore every factor in the browser, across three datasets, four return definitions and two sorts. Code The pipeline that builds the data and the library that forms the factors.

What is here

  • A daily bond panel from 2002-07, Enhanced, Standard and 144A TRACE combined, cleaned trade by trade, with no filter on par or dollar volume.
  • A monthly bond panel built from it, carrying returns, credit spreads, duration, ratings and the characteristics the literature sorts on.
  • 108 long-short factors, single sorted and within firm, published without sign correction and explorable in FactorViz.
  • Data reports that measure what each stage of the cleaning removed, generated by the run they document.

How the data is built

The pipeline is public and runs in four stages, at github.com/Alexander-M-Dickerson/trace-data-pipeline.

  • Stage 0 cleans the transaction tape, removing cancellations, corrections, reversals, interdealer double counts, decimal-shifted prices and bounce-back errors.
  • Stage 1 turns the cleaned trades into a daily panel with clean prices, accrued interest, yields, credit spreads, duration and convexity.
  • Stage 2 aggregates to the monthly panel and estimates the characteristics, betas and momentum signals.
  • Stage 3 reproduces every table and figure in the paper from that panel.

Most of a bond's return is the Treasury inside it

Over the past twenty years the greater part of the total return to holding corporate bonds is the return on the duration-matched Treasury portfolio, not compensation for credit or liquidity risk. That is the point of van Binsbergen, Nozawa and Schwert (2025), and the figure below is it, measured on our own data. All three duration-matched benchmarks we publish are here, because the split moves with the choice.

The corporate bond market, split into duration and credit Cumulative log return, value-weighted, each series compounded on its own. The Treasury leg is the duration-matched portfolio inside the market.

Every panel we publish carries all three duration-adjusted returns as columns, so this choice is yours to make rather than ours to make for you. The definitions are in FactorViz, under the About tab.

Priced risk in corporate bonds

This site also hosts the corrected Bai, Bali and Wen (2019) four factors, with the code and data to reproduce them, from Dickerson, Mueller and Robotti (2023).

The paper's central finding is that the bias-adjusted sample squared Sharpe ratio of the bond market portfolio is greater than that of all four BBW factors together. From an investment standpoint it is optimal to hold the market.
