Signals

The 108 corporate bond signals behind our factors. For each one, how it is formed, the line of our code that forms it, the paper it comes from and the months it covers.

Over all bonds, each factor is long the top decile of its signal and short the bottom decile. The within-firm sort ranks a bond only against other bonds of the same issuer.

When a noisy transaction price enters both a signal and the return that follows it, the errors in the two are correlated. This is the correlated errors-in-variables (CEIV) problem. In the TRACE data our price-based signals therefore use a price at least one business day before the price that starts the return. Each signal below says whether it is corrected this way.

Before TRACE, the full sample uses Lehman Brothers and ICE BofA quotes. Signals that need transaction data or daily returns start with TRACE, and so do signals estimated on a factor that starts with TRACE.
