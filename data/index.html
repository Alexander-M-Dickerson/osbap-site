Everything below is built by our own pipeline from raw TRACE, Lehman and ICE data, and is free to download. Sizes are measured from the files themselves.

The paper behind this data is under Revise and Resubmit, so the data may change over the coming months. Every change is recorded in the changelog on GitHub.

Monthly bond panel

One row per bond per month, from 2002-08. The main file carries returns, prices, ratings, duration and the standard characteristics. The additional file carries the wider set, including the beta, momentum and reversal blocks and the duration-adjusted returns. Together they are what the factors on this site are sorted from.

Daily bond panel

Every bond-day from 2002-07, combining Enhanced, Standard and 144A TRACE, with clean prices, accrued interest, yields, credit spreads, duration and convexity. No filter on par or dollar volume is applied, so small trades are present and can be filtered by the user rather than for them.

Notes on the files

  • Everything is zipped parquet unless the table says otherwise. Parquet reads in pandas with pd.read_parquet and in R with arrow::read_parquet.
  • Proprietary fields are excluded, so ratings are collapsed and identifiers such as gvkey are removed. Anyone with the underlying licences can regenerate the full version by running the pipeline.
  • Column definitions are in the daily data dictionary for the daily panel and the monthly data dictionary for the monthly panel.
  • To rebuild any of it from raw data, the whole pipeline is public at github.com/Alexander-M-Dickerson/trace-data-pipeline. It needs a WRDS account and the relevant data licences.
  • The daily and monthly panels are also distributed through WRDS as contributed data, daily and monthly.
