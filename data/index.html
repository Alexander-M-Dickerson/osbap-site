Notes on the files

  • Everything is zipped parquet unless the table says otherwise. Parquet reads in pandas with pd.read_parquet and in R with arrow::read_parquet.
  • No proprietary data is redistributed. gvkey and permco are blank, and the rating columns carry an investment-grade flag rather than the agency grade. Anyone holding the licences can merge their own on cusip, or regenerate the full version by running the pipeline.
  • Column definitions are in the daily data dictionary for the daily panel and the monthly data dictionary for the monthly panel.
  • To rebuild any of it from raw data, the whole pipeline is public at github.com/Alexander-M-Dickerson/trace-data-pipeline. It needs a WRDS account and the relevant data licences.
  • The daily and monthly panels are also distributed through WRDS as contributed data, daily and monthly.
