Corporate bond data

Corporate bond factors

108 long-short factors, 1973 to 2025, built from the combined Lehman-ICE and TRACE data. Over all bonds each factor is long the top decile and short the bottom decile. Within investment grade and within high yield it uses quintiles. Every factor is formed using two methods, a single sort and a within-firm sort, for all bonds, investment grade and high yield. The wide files hold the factor returns, and the panel files add the long and short legs, turnover and bond counts. FactorViz also has the TRACE-only and Lehman-ICE-only sets. The last file holds the bond market factors and the other monthly factors the pipeline uses to estimate bond betas.

Notes on the files

  • Files are zipped parquet unless stated otherwise. Read them with pd.read_parquet in Python or arrow::read_parquet in R.
  • No licensed data is redistributed. gvkey and permco are blank, and the rating columns hold an investment-grade flag (1 for investment grade, 11 otherwise) in place of the numeric rating (1 for AAA to 22 for D). With WRDS access and the licences you can merge your own ratings on cusip, or build the full version with the trace-data-pipeline.
  • The daily data dictionary and the monthly data dictionary define every column.
  • To rebuild the data from scratch, use the pipeline at github.com/Alexander-M-Dickerson/trace-data-pipeline. It needs a WRDS account and the data licences.
  • The daily and monthly panels are also on WRDS as contributed data (daily and monthly).
